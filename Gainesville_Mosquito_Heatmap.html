Method & sources

Built with the data-cleaning and visualization workflow from EMERGE Textbook 1: Data Analysis, applied to real NASA GLOBE Observer Mosquito Habitat Mapper reports (via UF GeoPlan/GeoDI's Florida layer). Experimental model, with heuristic parameters that require local trap-count calibration. Weather comes from a single county-center Open-Meteo location; it does not resolve neighborhood rainfall. Water distances use OSM feature centers, not shorelines. Reports are opportunistic, so no report does not mean no mosquitoes. Humidity, shade, drainage and control treatments are not measured. FALLBACK badges identify sample inputs. CDC mosquito life-cycle background.
